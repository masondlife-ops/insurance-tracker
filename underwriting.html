How the score works — read me

Final expense (PlanRight)
Level Full immediate death benefit day one — best case (Preferred or Standard).
Graded / Basic Reduced benefit first 2 years. Client still approvable, just not immediate.
Simplified issue / non-medical (Term, UL, WL)
Non-Med Standard Issues without a medical exam up to the product’s non-med face limit — if there’s no ratable impairment and build is in range.
Exam required Non-med path is blocked (an impairment, build, or face over the non-med limit). Goes to full underwriting — decision comes from labs/exam, so it’s not scored here.
Decline / Not issued Outside issue age, or a hard knockout on the guide.
Probability = confidence the client lands at (or above) the shown result, based on how far conditions/build sit from the cutoffs. It is an estimate to prioritize where to submit — not a guarantee of approval.
